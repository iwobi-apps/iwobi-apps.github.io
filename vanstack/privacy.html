VAN STACK

Privacy policy

Last updated: 9 November 2026

In short: Van Stack has no accounts and doesn't ask for your name or email. Your scores and progress stay on your phone. The game is free because it shows ads from Google AdMob, and Google collects some information to show and measure those ads. You decide whether those ads can be personalised.

Who we are

Van Stack is made by Adrian Iwobi, trading as Iwobi Apps, a sole trader in the United Kingdom. Adrian is responsible for your information (the "data controller"). Contact: adrianiwobi@googlemail.com.

What stays on your phone

The game saves the following on your device so you can carry on where you left off:

  • your best score, the level you've reached and your current run
  • your daily challenge results and streak
  • whether the sound is on and whether you've seen the tutorial

This is never sent to us or anyone else. Deleting the app deletes it.

Ads (Google AdMob)

Van Stack shows ads between some levels, and you can choose to watch an ad to carry on after the belt jams or for another go at the daily challenge. These ads come from Google AdMob. To show ads, stop fraud and count how often ads are seen, Google's software in the app collects information such as:

  • your device's advertising identifier (on iPhone, only if you allow tracking)
  • your IP address and approximate location (country or city, not your exact location)
  • the type of device and operating system, and the language setting
  • which ads are shown and whether you tap them

Google uses this as described in its own policy: how Google uses information from apps that use its services. Google may process it outside the UK, including in the United States, under the safeguards UK law requires.

Your choices about ads

  • Consent: if you're in the UK, the EU or another region where it's required, the game asks you the first time whether Google may use your information to personalise ads. If you say no, you still see ads, but they're not based on your interests. Google may still use limited information to show ads, prevent fraud and measure them.
  • iPhone tracking: the game asks whether it may track you across other companies' apps and websites. You can change this at any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • Android: you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's Settings → Google → Ads (or Settings → Privacy → Ads).
  • Changing your mind: tap Privacy choices at the bottom of the game's main menu at any time to change or withdraw your consent.

Why we're allowed to do this

Personalised ads are shown only with your consent, which you can withdraw at any time. Non-personalised ads, and the fraud prevention that comes with them, are based on our legitimate interest in keeping the game free to play.

What we don't do

  • We don't have accounts, and we don't collect your name, email address or contacts.
  • We don't sell your information.
  • We don't use your exact location.
  • There are no in-app purchases at the moment.

Children

Van Stack is not aimed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect information from them. If you think a child under 13 has used the game and you'd like their ad information removed, contact us and we'll help.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law you can ask to see, correct or delete information about you, or object to how it's used. As we don't hold any information that identifies you, most requests about ad information are handled by Google. Email adrianiwobi@googlemail.com and we'll help and reply within one month. If you're unhappy with how your information has been handled, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk).

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date at the top.
